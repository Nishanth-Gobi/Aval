To girl I love most 💚,
Happiest of birthdays

Got so much to tell you, All these bizzare stories, closely kept ones, stories that paint the portrait of my life, one Van Gogh - Picasso duet. Got so much to tell you, so much so, that, you might shut the ear, not wanting to hear so hear's an abridged version, of all the conversations that we never had, words that I never mouthed, letters, addressed to you, that I never wrote, for all the cheesy pickup lines that I never said, for the hickups and hickeys I didn't cause, this anthology of poems is for you!
