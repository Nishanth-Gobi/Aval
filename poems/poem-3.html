On a not so dark night

I am quite the storyteller. I would like to think so. And there's been a new narration lately! There's this young prince, one who had never lived in a castle. He's found building his first, a castle too big, too intricate and too intimate. The cold halls scare him, so does the demons held in the dungeon. And like any six year old, I am afraid the story would end, that I'll run out of pages, before the princess hops in! The classic story of how they've met. On a starry night, under an old bridge by the stables, dark from the shadows of the moonlight. But he knew it was her, of course, he knew, destiny wouldn't be much of a matchmaker if he didn't! Well, my story's a little different. It's set on a not so dark night, under a not so old bridge, with cars for horses, and the silence, well it was from the city going out, quietly, in a distance. You were nothing more than a blurry figure, afar, but hey, destiny said it was you, and I listened, the first time in many moons, And I cooked up, my story of romance, fine stories, they all got the same plot though, but you fall for me a little differently in each one. Well, what can I say, it's destiny!
