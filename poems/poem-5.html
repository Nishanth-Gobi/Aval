On our knees

We were both on our knees, and you've asked me "the question", and I stood there, quiet, like all the times from before. But this time, I am ready, I had been wanting to tell you, I had been waiting to tell you! "You see, from where I stand, I am the one with the question, and the answer, well, the answer was always yours!" And as you were overthinking things, ( you're cute when you do that ) I thought I'd help, "I don't want to make promises that I can't keep, So... If you want a happily ever after, please say no, If you a stable and mellow life, please say no, If you want a marriage and kids of your own someday, please say no, If your family wants a family guy, please say no, If you want a partner who'll put you before him, please say no, If you want a guaranteed companion, please say no, If you want someone to trust you from day one, please say no, ( for you'd have to teach me to trust you ) please say no, for I cannot give you any of those. please say no, for you're too good to have to compromise. These promises are the only ones I can keep, I promise you, it'll be one hell of a roller-coaster ride, I promise you that you'll never have to live the same day twice, I promise you, we can be plant moms together, I promise you, I'll try being your dad's friend and mom's bestie, I promise you, it'll be you before the world, in time, I promise you that when I am with you, it'll only be because I love you, and if we ever fall out of love, I will still be there for you, always. I say all this, as I see too many couples but too few lovers. And I want really want us to be the later Say yes, and I promise you that there'll be a lot less 'I's and a lot more 'we's, in time. Say yes, and I'll love with all my heart, not unconditionally but with the one that you'd love me too on my darkest days"
